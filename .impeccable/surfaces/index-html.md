## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Approval is a relay, not a black box. The expanded surface preserves one accountable invoice story while making the surrounding Demo51 product map familiar enough for self-guided exploration.

**OWN-WORLD:** Deep green navigation, sand workspace, white evidence cards, lime action and success, and restrained lavender for the active approval role. Gellix-style geometric type, 1px neutral rules, compact iconography, and layered evidence cards make the page recognizably Rillion with all copy removed.

**STORY:** A prospect immediately sees a recognizable six-step Guided tour control, then sees work arrive in To Verify and moves next into the Invoice Log, where the live field order, flow/accounting proposals, and labeled green/yellow/red evidence explain readiness. Fully matched purchase orders and contracts route directly to recording; non-PO invoices instead show AI-generated flow and account-posting proposals. The prospect then inspects source evidence, selects an Approval role, and reviews that role's decisions. Documents and Contracts each provide a register-to-detail click-through. Payments carries approved work through ready, awaiting, in-progress, and completed states before eleven Rillion Analytics boards grouped in platform order. Every interaction explains state, ownership, payment progress, or the next board.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A Demo51-style grouped navigation rail with the official lime Rillion logo anchors the product. The dashboard keeps the task queue, selected invoice, approval relay, and evidence together; the upper-right role selector makes approval ownership available from every view.

**FORM:** Approval relay remains the primary operational composition. Secondary pages use one dominant table or report stage instead of equal-card dashboards; Documents and Contracts pair semantic register links with three-part source/metadata/flow detail views, while Analytics pairs a grouped board index with one evidence-rich screenshot.

**FINISH:** Unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
