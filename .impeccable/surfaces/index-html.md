## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Approval is a relay, not a black box. The expanded surface preserves one accountable invoice story while making the surrounding Demo51 product map familiar enough for self-guided exploration.

**OWN-WORLD:** Deep green navigation, sand workspace, white evidence cards, lime action and success, and restrained lavender for the active approval role. Gellix-style geometric type, 1px neutral rules, compact iconography, and layered evidence cards make the page recognizably Rillion with all copy removed.

**STORY:** A prospect immediately receives a Guided tour chooser for Accounts payable, Invoice approver, Finance leader, or the full platform, plus five module shortcuts and an obvious free-exploration exit. One Precision Tools invoice connects Capture, Invoice Log, manager approval, and Payments. Shareable scenario links land directly in the relevant role and module. Capture opens invoice detail, coding, and settings; destination-labeled Back controls return detail screens to their owning list or prior Capture screen. Payments carries approved work through ready, awaiting, in-progress, and completed states before eleven Analytics boards; three boards are interactive synthetic views and every product reference loads lazily. The completed tour offers the official live-demo handoff.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A centered deep-green Guided tour chooser opens over an inert Demo51-style shell on every fresh load. Its role choices, full-platform action, direct module shortcuts, and free-exploration exit make the next choice explicit; focus remains contained until dismissal, then returns to the launcher.

**FORM:** Approval relay remains the primary operational composition. Secondary pages use one dominant table or report stage instead of equal-card dashboards; Capture moves from a dense queue into a source/fields/coding workspace and then field settings, Documents and Contracts pair semantic register links with three-part source/metadata/flow detail views, and Analytics pairs a grouped board index with either one interactive synthetic board or one lazy-loaded reference screen.

**FINISH:** Unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
