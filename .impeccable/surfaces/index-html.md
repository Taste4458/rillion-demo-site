## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Approval is a relay, not a black box. The expanded surface preserves one accountable invoice story while making the surrounding Demo51 product map familiar enough for self-guided exploration.

**OWN-WORLD:** Deep green navigation, sand workspace, white evidence cards, lime action and success, and restrained lavender for the active approval role. Gellix-style geometric type, 1px neutral rules, compact iconography, and layered evidence cards make the page recognizably Rillion with all copy removed.

**STORY:** A prospect immediately receives a Guided tour welcome with one clear start action, five platform shortcuts, and an obvious free-exploration exit. The tour then moves through To Verify, Invoice Log, approvals, Payments, and Analytics, with Back available during the walkthrough. Capture opens invoice detail, coding, and settings; destination-labeled Back controls return every detail screen to its owning list or prior Capture screen. The Invoice Log's live field order, flow/accounting proposals, and labeled green/yellow/red evidence explain readiness. Fully matched purchase orders and contracts route directly to recording; non-PO invoices instead show AI-generated flow and account-posting proposals. Documents and Contracts each provide a register-to-detail click-through. Payments carries approved work through ready, awaiting, in-progress, and completed states before eleven Rillion Analytics boards grouped in platform order.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A centered deep-green Guided tour welcome opens over the Demo51-style shell on every fresh load. Its lime primary action, direct module shortcuts, and free-exploration exit make the next choice explicit; dismissing it reveals the grouped navigation rail, official lime Rillion logo, task queue, selected invoice, approval relay, evidence, and upper-right role selector.

**FORM:** Approval relay remains the primary operational composition. Secondary pages use one dominant table or report stage instead of equal-card dashboards; Capture moves from a dense queue into a source/fields/coding workspace and then field settings, Documents and Contracts pair semantic register links with three-part source/metadata/flow detail views, and Analytics pairs a grouped board index with one evidence-rich screenshot.

**FINISH:** Unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
